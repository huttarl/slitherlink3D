📖 How to Play

Goal: Draw a single loop along the edges of the polyhedron.

Instructions:

  • The loop must be a single continuous path, but cannot touch itself.
  • Numbers show exactly how many segments of the loop must touch that face.
  • Click edges to mark them as part of the loop (dark blue), not part of the loop (white), or unknown (gray).
  • Drag to rotate. Scroll to zoom.
  • More info and strategy tips
